# Unslop

Identify audience, purpose, and meaning before editing. Preserve facts, technical names, necessary caveats, quotations, and intentional tone. Mark unsupported claims instead of inventing their evidence.

Scan by function rather than banning isolated words:

- Replace vague significance, unattributed authority, and decorative participial clauses with a source, mechanism, or concrete result.
- Replace inflated vocabulary, abstract metaphor, and indirect substitutes for "is" with literal language where clearer.
- Remove forced contrasts, artificial triads, false ranges, synonym cycling, and conclusions that repeat the opening.
- Remove flattery, chatbot offers, filler, redundant hedging, and adverbs standing in for facts.
- Reduce decorative formatting, redundant bold labels, title-case headings, emojis, and curly quotation marks in ordinary authored prose.
- Split dense clauses, restore missing articles and verbs, and name the actor when passive voice hides responsibility.

Use whole sentences without converting the draft into compressed notes. Replace em dashes with periods or commas. Preserve meaningful punctuation inside quotations, code, and exact interfaces. A necessary technical term is not a defect because it appears in a pattern list.

Read the revision for factual drift, lost nuance, and mechanical rhythm. Return the requested artifact, with explanations only when needed or requested.
